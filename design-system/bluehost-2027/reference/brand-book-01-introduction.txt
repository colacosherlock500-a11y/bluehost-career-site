Our WordPress Legacy and Messaging
Bluehost has been and will always be committed to supporting the future of WordPress and the open web broadly. We have been recommended by WordPress.org, the non-profit Foundation that governs open-source WordPress, since 2005. ​ ​ We are partnered with Automattic, the for-profit company founded and led by WordPress co-founder Matt Mullenweg. Automattic's WP Cloud infrastructure fuels our Agency Cloud offerings and we jointly take the solution to market.​
The Bluehost Group family of brands includes Bluehost together with: ​ Yoast, one the world's best and most user-friendly SEO plugins for WordPress.​ YITH, one of the largest collections of WooCommerce plugins for WordPress highly regarded for ease of use as well.​ HostGator, developer-friendly hosting platform known for freedom and flexibility. ​ ​ Bluehost today is for people who are building anything, anywhere. We are seizing the market opportunity with AI Agent and App creation, providing high-performance hosting services beyond WordPress. As such, we are leading with broader, more inclusive messaging that also includes WordPress.  ​
