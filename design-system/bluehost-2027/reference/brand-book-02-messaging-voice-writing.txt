Eyebrows
The eyebrow is often used for SEO, and we use it as our H1.  We don’t always use eyebrows, but they’re always: All caps Simple and straightforward
Headlines
Headlines should be clever  and eye-catching, convincing people to keep reading. Headlines are: Written in sentence case Strong on their own, not requiring exclamation points or periods
Subheads
Our subheads provide context and keywords in support of the headline, so while they’re concise, they should also be descriptive. Subheads are: Written in sentence case Use proper punctuation, typically avoiding all caps and  exclamation points
CTAs
The CTA should be: Short and sweet Title case Under 16 characters when possible
