Smooth gradients
Built natively in Figma with no blurred shapes: a two- or three-stop linear base plus one or two large, soft radial glows that fade to transparent. Every transition is continuous, so there are no splotches. Colors come from neighbouring shades and every opaque stop is linked to a Bluehost colors variable.
